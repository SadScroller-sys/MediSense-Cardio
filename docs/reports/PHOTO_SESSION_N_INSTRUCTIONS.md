# Photo session N: 60 pages, 120 photos (about 1 hour)

**Why.** Our two new disease-finding models (A and B) could not be tested on phone photos, because we had too few photos with the diseases in them. This session fixes that. The 60 pages are real ECGs with known findings, drawn as ECG paper pages. You photograph them from a laptop screen. We then test the models once on your photos.

**You do not need to know which page has which disease.** The viewer shows the pages in a random order, and we match every photo to its page by the picture itself (as we did for the printed-paper photos).

## What you need
- The laptop and the phone you want to use. Tell me the phone model (the report should say it).
- A dim or normal room. No sunlight falling on the screen. Wipe the screen.
- Screen brightness at about 80 to 100%. Turn off any night-light or blue-light filter.

## Steps
1. Open this file in the laptop's browser (Chrome or Edge): `D:\Capstone Project\results\paper_n\photo_session\index.html`
2. Press **F** for full screen. The page fills the screen. Press **H** to hide the black bar if it is in the way.
3. For each page, take **2 photos with the phone camera**:
   - **Photo 1, straight:** hold the phone in front of the screen, about 30 to 40 cm away, level, so the **whole page is in the picture** (with a little dark edge around it).
   - **Photo 2, tilted:** move to the side so the phone looks at the screen at about 30 degrees, and keep the **whole page in the picture**.
   - Hold the phone **either way, landscape or upright**. Both are fine.
4. Press the **right arrow** or the **space bar** for the next page. There are 60 pages. The viewer says "Page 7 of 60".
5. At the end you have about 120 photos.

## Rules (please keep them, they keep the test honest)
- **Do not edit the photos.** No filters, no crop, no HDR or "beauty" mode, no flash.
- **Do not retake a photo because the result might look bad.** If a photo is blurred or half out of the picture, retake it right away, but do not look at any result.
- If you skip a page by mistake, go back with the left arrow.
- Send the **original files**, not WhatsApp pictures. If you use WhatsApp, choose **"Document"** to keep full quality. A cable, Google Drive or the phone's "Share" to the laptop also work.
