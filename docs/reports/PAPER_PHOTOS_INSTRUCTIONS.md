# Printed-paper photo test: what to do (simple steps)

Why: so far every real photo was of a laptop screen. A patient's photo is usually of **printed paper**. This test checks our straightening fix and our traffic light on paper. It is the most valuable test we can still add. Rules were written first: `docs/plan/analysis_plan_changes.md`, "Section H".

You need: a printer, 20 sheets of plain paper, the phone you used before, a table, and about 45 minutes.

## Step 1. Print

1. Open this file: `results/paper_h/ECG_pages_H01_H20_PRINT.pdf` (20 pages, H01 to H20).
2. Print it on **plain paper**. Settings: **landscape**, **Fit to page**, **colour** if your printer can (the pink grid helps the tool), one page per sheet.
3. Keep the sheets in order: H01 on top, H20 at the bottom. Do not write on them, fold them or staple them.
4. If a sheet prints badly (blank strip, cut-off), print that page again. Do not fix it by hand.

## Step 2. Take the photos (40 photos, 2 for each sheet)

Use the **same phone** as before. Normal camera mode. **No flash, no filters, no zoom tricks.**

For each sheet, in order H01, H02, ... H20:

1. Put the sheet **flat on a table** in good, even light (daylight from a window is best; no shadow of your hand or phone on the page).
2. **Photo 1 (straight):** hold the phone straight above the sheet. The whole sheet must be inside the picture, with a little table visible around it.
3. **Photo 2 (angled):** stay at the same distance, and tilt the phone about 30 degrees to one side (as if you are looking at the sheet from the side). The whole sheet must still be inside the picture.
4. Check the **photo only for these mistakes**: page cut off, finger on the page, completely blurred photo. If you see one of these, **retake it straight away**, before moving to the next sheet. Do not retake anything later.

Order matters. The tool matches photos to sheets by the order of the file names: **sheet 1 straight, sheet 1 angled, sheet 2 straight, sheet 2 angled, ...** Take them in exactly this order and do not skip any.

## Step 3. Send the photos

1. Copy all 40 photos to **one new folder** (for example `Downloads\Paper_Photos`). It must contain only these 40 photos.
2. Do not rename, edit, crop or rotate them.
3. Tell me the folder name. I will digitise them and run the test **once**.

## Please do not

- look at the results of any photo before all 40 are given (the tool does not show results until I run the test, so there is nothing to see);
- retake or replace photos after you have given them;
- choose "better" sheets: use all 20 in order.

## Optional: a second phone

If you can, ask a friend to photograph the **same 20 printed sheets** with a different phone, in the same way (40 more photos, own folder). That tells us whether the result depends on your phone. It is counted separately and never mixed with your phone's photos.

## What the test will say (so you know)

- If our straightening fix still cuts bad readings on printed paper, the main claim becomes much stronger.
- If it does not, we report that as it is. It is still a real result and goes into the limits.
- Either way it is reported as measured: a good result and a bad result are both honest findings.
