# References, Chicago author-date style (for the author-year Word version only)

Our IEEE paper (`main_v2.tex`) uses numbered references and does not need this file. Use this list only if the paper is typeset in author-year style.
In the text write: Jabbour et al. 2025 (two authors: Kwon and Kim 2026); in brackets: (Jabbour et al. 2025; Kwon and Kim 2026). No comma between name and year.

Notes on what was fixed compared with the pasted list:
- One style for every entry (year after the author list, followed by a full stop; "and" before the last author, never "&").
- "Deepanshi" is a single name, with no initial. The stray "J." and "A." were removed. The "A." belongs to Mehri Shervedani.
- Details and DOIs added from Crossref records (checked 17 Nov 2026). Preprints without a DOI keep their arXiv or engrXiv number.
